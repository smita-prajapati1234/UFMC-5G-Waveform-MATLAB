# Waveform Comparison Study

## Overview

This project presents a comparative study of different multicarrier waveforms used in modern wireless communication systems.
The study compares the performance characteristics of different waveform techniques using simulation-based analysis. The comparison focuses on important parameters relevant to 5G and beyond-5G wireless communication systems.

## Waveforms Studied
The study includes comparative analysis of:
- OFDM
- UFMC
- FBMC
- GFDM

## Publication Details
This research work was presented at an academic conference.

**Paper Title:**  
Design Aspects and Performance Analysis of Multicarrier Waveform Contenders of 5G and Beyond Wireless Networks
